ATV RX beta

A DVB-S2 receiver for amateur television on 13 cm that fits in a pocket: a Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 on USB-OTG is the radio, an Android phone or tablet demodulates, decodes and shows the picture with MER, constellation and stream information. Its counterpart is the stand-alone ESP32-C3 beacon of ESP32-DATV-SA – and the app also transmits: with a XIAO ESP32-C3 on USB-OTG it sends a test card or the phone's camera live as DVB-S2.

Přijímač DVB-S2 pro amatérskou televizi v pásmu 13 cm: XIAO ESP32-S3 na USB-OTG jako rádio, telefon nebo tablet s Androidem demoduluje, dekóduje a ukazuje obraz s MER, konstelací a údaji o streamu. S XIAO ESP32-C3 aplikace i vysílá – testkartu nebo obraz z kamery.
